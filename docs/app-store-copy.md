# App Store Listing Draft

## App Name
CheaperFind

## Subtitle
Find products cheaper with AI

## Description
CheaperFind helps shoppers upload a product photo or paste a shopping link to discover exact matches, similar styles, and cheaper alternatives across online stores. Save favorite finds, compare prices, and shop smarter.

## Keywords
shopping, deals, fashion, dupes, price comparison, AI, clothes, style, marketplace

## Privacy summary
CheaperFind may collect account information, uploaded images, product URLs, saved items, payment status, and analytics. Uploaded product photos are used to identify shopping attributes and improve search results.
